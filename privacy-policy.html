Privacy Policy

MateEazy · Last updated: 3 November 2026

MateEazy ("MateEazy", "we", "us") is a mobile app that helps people find rooms and roommates nearby. This policy explains what information we collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. The service is operated by Pranay Koley, Kolkata, West Bengal, India. Contact: support@mateeazy.in.

1. Who can use MateEazy

MateEazy is for people aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has an account, contact us and we will remove it.

3. How we use information

  • Provide, personalise and secure the service (matching, discovery, chat, notifications).
  • Show your profile and listings to other users as you choose.
  • Moderate content, investigate reports and enforce our Terms of Service.
  • Comply with law and protect our users and service.

We do not sell your personal information, and the app does not show third-party advertising or use advertising identifiers.

4. What other users can see

Your name, age, profile photo, bio, occupation, lifestyle answers and listings are visible to other signed-in users. Chat messages are visible only to the people in the conversation. You can block a user at any time; blocked users cannot message you and you will not see each other's rooms.

5. Who we share information with

  • Google (Firebase Authentication, Firebase Cloud Messaging, Google Sign-In) – sign-in and push notifications.
  • Google Maps Platform / Places – maps and place search. Text you type into location search and map positions may be sent to Google.
  • Our hosting provider – stores the data described above on our servers.
  • Authorities – if required by law or to protect safety.

These providers process data under their own privacy terms. We do not share your data with data brokers.

6. Security

We use access controls and encrypted storage for credentials on your device. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can disable your account (hides it; signing in again restores it) or delete it from Settings → Delete account, or follow the steps at the account deletion page. After a 30-day grace period (during which signing in again restores your account) we permanently delete your sign-in account (Firebase Authentication), profile details, profile photo, listings and listing photos, comments, likes, block lists, notification token and saved location, and all of your chat conversations and the messages in them, including messages you sent and received. Because a conversation is shared, deleting your account removes the conversation for the other participant as well. Server logs are kept for a limited time for security and abuse prevention and then removed. Information we are legally required to keep may be retained only for as long as the law requires.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Edit your profile and lifestyle answers at any time in the app.
  • Turn location access off in your phone settings; choose which notifications you receive in Settings → Notification settings.
  • Request access, correction or deletion of your data by emailing support@mateeazy.in. Depending on where you live (for example India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, or the EU/UK GDPR) you may have further rights, including to withdraw consent and to complain to a data-protection authority.

9. International transfers

Our providers may process data in countries other than your own. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. We will change the date above and, for material changes, tell you in the app.
