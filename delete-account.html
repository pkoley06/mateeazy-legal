Delete your MateEazy account

MateEazy · Last updated: 3 November 2026

Option 1 – In the app (fastest)

  1. Open MateEazy and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Tap the settings button, then choose Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is hidden immediately and signs you out.

Option 2 – If you can't open the app

Email support@mateeazy.in from the email address on your account (or tell us the phone number you signed in with) with the subject "Delete my MateEazy account". We will confirm and process your request within 7 days.

What happens next

  • For 30 days (grace period) your profile is hidden. If you sign in again during this time, your account is restored. Nothing is deleted yet.
  • After the grace period we permanently delete: your sign-in account (Firebase Authentication), profile details, profile photo, lifestyle answers, your room listings and their photos, your comments and likes, your block lists, your notification token and saved location, and all of your chat conversations and messages. Because a conversation is shared, it is removed for the other participant too.
  • Security server logs are kept for a limited time and then removed. We keep nothing else unless the law requires it, and only for as long as the law requires.
  • After deletion you can sign up again at any time, but you will start with a new, empty account.

Questions? See our Privacy Policy or email support@mateeazy.in.
